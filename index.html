Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

DigitraArtworks Content Scheduler is a private application used exclusively by its owner to create, schedule and manage original content on the DigitraArtworks Pinterest business account.

Information We Access

The application may access Pinterest account information, boards, Pins and authentication tokens necessary to publish and manage content through the official Pinterest API.

How Information Is Used

Information is used only to authenticate the account, select boards, create Pins, schedule content and prevent duplicate publishing.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share personal information with third parties. Data is only transmitted to Pinterest as required to provide the application's publishing functions.

Data Storage and Security

Access credentials and tokens are stored securely and are not made publicly available. Data is retained only for as long as necessary to operate the application.

Data Deletion

Access can be revoked through the connected Pinterest account. Related locally stored account information and authentication credentials can also be permanently deleted upon request.

Cookies and Tracking

This privacy policy page does not use advertising cookies or tracking technologies.
